Proposed areas of work

Format Kitchen

Proposed

Conversion tools that move annotation and corpus data from one format to another. Start from CoNLL, XML, or RDF, pick a target, and follow a clear recipe so students and researchers can interchange data without hand-writing transforms.

Interop · conversion

Annotation tools

Proposed

A family of helpers for tagging, glossing, and scheme mapping. Edit morphological and syntactic layers, check tagsets against reference models, and keep annotation workflows transparent enough to teach and reuse.

Tagging · glossing · schemes

Parallel corpora visualizer

Proposed

Aligned multilingual reading for parallel corpora. Compare verse or sentence units across languages side by side, follow alignment links, and optionally overlay lexicon hits while you read.

Parallel text · alignment

SPARQL from human language

Proposed

Ask questions in ordinary language and turn them into SPARQL over linked linguistic data. Aimed at small hosted graphs so students can explore LLOD resources without writing query syntax first.

Query · LLOD
